# OneFit – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

OneFit Gym L.L.C. is a premium fitness and wellness facility located at The Ivy by Metrical, Jumeirah Village Triangle, Dubai. It offers gym facilities, strength training, HIIT, boxing, Reformer Pilates, yoga, recovery facilities, and children's fitness programmes.

The redesigned homepage positions OneFit as an integrated fitness and wellness destination rather than a conventional gym. It focuses on communicating the full membership experience, helping visitors discover relevant activities, and encouraging membership enquiries and free-pass conversions.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Brand Positioning and First Impression
- Strengthened the homepage messaging around premium fitness, wellness, and community.
- Improved the hero section to communicate the overall OneFit experience more clearly.
- Created a stronger visual introduction to differentiate OneFit from conventional gyms.
- Addressed the previous lack of impact in communicating the brand's broader fitness and wellness positioning.

### 2. Better Organisation of Services and Experiences
- Structured the main offerings into clearer fitness and wellness categories.
- Improved the presentation of gym training, HIIT, boxing, Reformer Pilates, yoga, recovery, and kids' fitness.
- Made different activities easier to explore based on visitors' interests.
- Addressed the fragmented presentation of services and created a clearer discovery journey.

### 3. Stronger Membership Conversion Journey
- Gave greater visual priority to the free-pass offer and membership options.
- Improved the connection between membership information, promotional offers, and enquiry calls to action.
- Made the next steps towards joining OneFit easier to identify.
- Addressed the previous lack of clear conversion prioritisation across the homepage.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Established a stronger premium fitness aesthetic to reflect OneFit's positioning and community-focused identity.
- **Homepage Layout:** Improved section hierarchy to guide visitors from brand introduction to service discovery and membership information.
- **Service Navigation:** Organised fitness, specialist classes, recovery, and family programmes into a more understandable structure.
- **User Experience:** Created a clearer journey for visitors exploring facilities, activities, and membership opportunities.
- **Conversion Focus:** Increased the prominence of free-pass and membership-related calls to action.
- **Brand Consistency:** Connected the visual presentation and messaging around fitness, wellness, recovery, and community.

---

**Project Focus:** Stronger premium fitness positioning, organised service discovery, and a more visible membership conversion journey.
